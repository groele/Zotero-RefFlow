### Cache settings

Open **Settings → Zotero RefFlow → Storage** in the retrieval section. The master switch and the PDF/API source switches control persistent list reuse. Complete online references and citation/related lists each default to one day, complete PDF lists default to retention until manual refresh, and partial lists default to 15 minutes. A duration of zero days keeps a complete list until its source signature changes, manual refresh replaces it, or storage limits evict it. Partial results accept 1–10,080 minutes. Turning storage off preserves existing snapshots for later use.
